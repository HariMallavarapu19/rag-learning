Employee Handbook

Company Overview

Our company provides software products and technology services
to customers around the world. Employees are expected to work
professionally and collaborate effectively with their teams.

Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday.
Employees should normally be available during their team's
scheduled working hours.

Annual Leave

Employees receive 20 days of annual leave every calendar year.
Employees can carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days
into the following year.

Leave Requests

Employees should submit annual leave requests through the HR
system at least 3 working days before the requested leave date.
Emergency leave may be requested without the normal advance notice.

Health Insurance

Employees who complete at least one year of service are eligible
for the company's health insurance program. The company provides
health insurance coverage for employees and eligible dependents.

Retirement Benefits

The company provides a retirement savings program. The company
contributes 5 percent of an employee's eligible salary when the
employee contributes at least 5 percent.

Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to
their manager's approval. Employees working remotely must remain
available during normal working hours.

Company Equipment

Approved remote employees may receive company equipment such as
laptops and monitors. Employees are responsible for taking care
of company equipment and reporting technical problems to IT.

Information Security

Employees must follow company information security policies when
accessing company systems. Company accounts must not be shared
with other people.

Performance Reviews

Employees participate in periodic performance reviews with their
managers. Performance reviews are used to discuss achievements,
goals, development opportunities, and areas for improvement.

Employee Expenses

Employees may be reimbursed for approved business expenses.
Employees should submit receipts and expense reports according to
the company's expense procedures.
