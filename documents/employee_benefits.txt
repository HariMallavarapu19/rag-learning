Employee Benefits Policy

Employees who complete at least one year of service are eligible
for the company's health insurance program.

The company provides health insurance coverage for employees
and eligible dependents.

Employees may also participate in the retirement savings program.

The company contributes 5 percent of the employee's eligible salary
to the retirement savings program when the employee contributes
at least 5 percent.

Employees can contact the HR benefits team for questions about
insurance and retirement benefits.
