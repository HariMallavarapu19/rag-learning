Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week,
subject to their manager's approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during
their normal working hours.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet
connection while working remotely.

Company equipment such as laptops and monitors may be provided
for approved remote employees.

Employees must follow the company's information security policies
when accessing company systems from outside the office.
